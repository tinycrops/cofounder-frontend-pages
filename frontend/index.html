Central hypothesis

Domain-specific superintelligence may arrive as compact, inspectable artifacts.

Hypothesis H1

High-value AI systems in constrained domains will increasingly be delivered as portable artifacts that combine a bounded interface, local model execution, and explicit policy or knowledge constraints.

Hypothesis H2

The winning architecture will separate three layers: a tiny model, a verifiable orchestration graph, and a user-facing shell compiled to a distribution format with unusually broad portability.

Hypothesis H3

In regulated or narrow operational contexts, artifact quality will be measured less by general conversational breadth and more by traceability, reproducibility, and efficiency per byte.

Research anchor

Two Princeton papers now sit at the center of this site.

Empirical paper

Knowledge Graphs are Implicit Reward Models

The evidence paper. It trains a Qwen3 14B model on short-hop medical reasoning and uses knowledge-graph path signals during RL to improve zero-shot generalization to longer-hop tasks.

What is evidence here?

The strongest evidence is narrow and specific: structured path-derived reward signals appear to improve specialist compositional reasoning on a medical benchmark more effectively than scale alone.

What is still speculative?

The bigger DSS vision is still an architectural hypothesis. The “Pocket Sage,” labor-market, and infrastructure arguments are compelling, but they remain broader extrapolations rather than experimentally settled facts.

Our synthesis

Paper one gives the mechanism. Paper two gives the system design. This website explores what happens if we take both seriously and build artifact-native, audit-friendly user layers around them.

Proposed architecture

The artifact-native DSS stack

01

Policy graph

A knowledge graph, protocol graph, or task graph constrains what the system is allowed to infer, reveal, or recommend.

02

Tiny domain model

A compact model trained under parameter-golf-like pressure performs narrow-domain ranking, summarization, and next-step generation.

03

Compiled interaction shell

A Fontemon-like or other artifact shell exposes only auditable state transitions and human-readable outputs.

04

Portable distribution

The result ships as a tiny, movable object: a font, package, local app bundle, document-like executable medium, or edge-native file.

Scientific claim

The economically dominant AI systems of the next phase may not look like monolithic chat endpoints. They may look like compact task-specific artifacts with strict interfaces, embedded intelligence, and verifiable behavior envelopes.

Falsifiable predictions

What would make this hypothesis stronger or weaker?

Prediction A

Within 24 months, more high-trust workflows will ship with bounded, local, or semi-local models instead of pure cloud calls.

Prediction B

Artifact size budgets will become an explicit benchmark axis in more model competitions and product evaluations.

Prediction C

User interfaces for narrow domains will increasingly be generated from policy graphs rather than hand-written from scratch.

Prediction D

If tiny domain models fail to maintain useful quality under severe compression, this theory weakens substantially.

The strongest alternative to the black-box cloud generalist may be a glass-box artifact: small enough to move, narrow enough to trust, and structured enough to audit.
